The Story of North Star Bakery

Founded in 2015, North Star Bakery was created to bring traditional, slow-fermented artisan baking back to our neighborhood. What started as a small booth at the local farmers market has grown into a warm, community-centered storefront where neighbors gather every morning.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts with honest ingredients. We source 100% of our flour from local regional mills, use organic dairy, and never add artificial preservatives or additives to any of our doughs.
